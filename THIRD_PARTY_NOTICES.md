## ISO 20022 schema

`schemas/pain.001.001.03.xsd` and `schemas/pain.001.001.09.xsd` are ISO 20022
message schemas generated by the SWIFT Standards Workstation / Standards
Editor. ISO 20022 and the schemas are not authored by this project and are not
relicensed under the project's MIT license.

Schema SHA-256:
`677cb4b1152ee49b379ea063485e36c3701d3427b144d5c7607dfa77819a0866`

Generic pain.001.001.09 schema SHA-256:
`05440a7e84f695e7dbc6677082f43623cd7393881b76a5e8d648ae21d7d728e3`

The ISO 20022 website was unavailable when the `.09` schema was added. Its
contents were cross-checked against byte-identical public copies in
[`php-sepa-xml`](https://github.com/php-sepa-xml/php-sepa-xml) and
[`OCA/bank-payment`](https://github.com/OCA/bank-payment), plus an identical
copy with CRLF line endings in
[`python-sepaxml`](https://github.com/raphaelm/python-sepaxml).

Before redistributing a fork, verify the current ISO 20022/SWIFT terms that
apply to the schema. If redistribution is not desired, remove the schema and
its generated `data/schemas-data.js`; the reader will continue to display XML
but XSD validation will be reported as unavailable.
